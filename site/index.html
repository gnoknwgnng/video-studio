Script & scenes

Scene types and their fields
problem
Icon storm + up to 4 pain cards that flip in on cue words.
kicker, title, title_em, alert_word, icon (tooth, document, heart, shield, building, box, chip), meter_label, cards[{text, cue}]
stats
Animated bars + "1 in N" people callout.
label, bars[{name, value, display, cue}], callout{em, text, people, empty, cue, empty_cue}
intro
Big logo, tagline, 0–2 photos, audience tags.
logo_cue, tagline, tagline_em, tagline_cue, photos[], tags[{text, cue, highlight}]
steps
2–4 numbered steps on a timeline with chips.
title, steps[{title, chips[], cue}]
flip
A card that gets stamped, then flips to the fix.
title, title_em, front{kicker, title, rows[{k, v, style}], stamp, cue}, back{kicker, title, rows[{k, v, style, cue}]} · style: alert, ok, primary
question
"So what makes X different?"
pre, em, post, em_cue, mark_cue
compare
Up to 4 rows: others vs you.
left_label, right_label, rows[{left, right, left_cue, right_cue}]
end
Optional shrinking bar chart, logo, tagline, URL.
chart{label, bars[{label, from, to}]}, tagline, tagline_em, logo_cue, tagline_cue
lines (every scene)
What the voice says in that scene.
[{say, sub, seed}] · say is spoken, sub is the subtitle (defaults to say). Spell names how they sound, e.g. "Docs, Docs". Change seed to re-roll a line.
cue words
A word from that scene's lines. The animation fires the moment the voice says it.
  1. From a topic: type a topic and press Make the video. The AI writes the script (saved as videos/<topic>/video.json so you can edit it later) and the workflow builds the video.
  2. Write my own: fill in the form and script, then press Create on GitHub. GitHub opens with videos/<folder>/video.json filled in; press Commit changes.
  3. The commit starts the Make video workflow in the Actions tab. It writes the voice with Chatterbox, times every animation to the spoken words, adds sound design and renders the MP4.
  4. About 15–20 minutes for the first run of a new video (voice on CPU), then about 5 minutes when you only change visuals: voice lines are cached in the repo.
  5. The finished MP4 appears in the Gallery here and under Releases on GitHub.
  6. To change a video, edit its video.json on GitHub and commit. To rebuild without changes: Actions → Make video → Run workflow.
