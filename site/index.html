Script & scenes

Scene types and their fields
photo
One full-screen real photo with a short caption.
kicker, caption, caption_em, image_query or photo
gallery
2–4 photo tiles that pop in as each is named.
title, tiles[{label, icon, image_query, cue}]
chart
Bar, column, line, donut or pie chart built as the numbers are spoken.
kind, title, unit, source, highlight, data[{label, value, display, icon, cue}]
flow
A process diagram: icon nodes joined by arrows.
title, nodes[{label, sub, icon, cue}]
icons
3–6 big icons with short labels.
title, items[{label, icon, cue}]
versus
Two sides with photos and short points.
left{label, image_query, points[], cue}, right{...}
map
World map that zooms to the countries named, with pins.
title, places[{country, label, value, cue}]
spotlight
A big line-drawn icon; related icons fly in and orbit it; shakes red (problem) or glows (win). Best opener.
kicker, title, icon, satellites[{icon, label, cue}], mood: bad|good, climax_cue, caption
pictogram
"1 in 5" as a grid of people or icons.
total, highlight, icon, display, label, label_em, cue
kpis
2–4 key numbers counting up with icons.
items[{value, prefix, suffix, label, icon, cue}]
bubbles
Circles sized by value to compare sizes.
items[{label, value, display, icon, cue}], unit, note
gauge
A meter with a needle: score, rating, risk.
value (0–100), suffix, zones[], label, label_em, cue
funnel
Stages that narrow (visitors → buyers).
stages[{label, value, display, icon, cue}]
cycle
A repeating loop of 3–6 steps.
center, nodes[{label, icon, cue}]
chat
A phone conversation, bubble by bubble.
name, messages[{from: me|them, text, cue}] · give each line its own voice
reveal
Before/after photos with a sliding wipe.
before{label, image_query}, after{...}, caption
video options
transition: wipe, flash, none · progress_bar: true/false · background_motion: true/false
icon (any item)
Any English word or one of 2,292 Lucide icons, e.g. smartphone, landmark, truck.
hook
A big statement that appears word by word as it is spoken. Best opener.
kicker, text, em, highlight
problem
Icon storm + up to 4 pain cards that flip in on cue words.
kicker, title, title_em, alert_word, icon (document, phone, globe, rocket, bulb, user, chart, coin, book, home, cap, chat, bolt, cloud, lock, briefcase, bag, leaf, mail, cross, heart, shield, building, box, chip, tooth), meter_label, cards[{text, cue}]
number
One giant number that counts up on cue (or a % ring).
kicker, value, prefix, suffix, percent, label, label_em, note, cue
timeline
3–5 dated events lighting up on a line.
title, events[{date, text, cue}]
list
3–6 numbered items (tips, top N, mistakes).
kicker, title, items[{text, note, cue}], countdown
quote
A real quote with its author.
text, author, role, cue
broll
Full-screen footage or photo with a caption. Stock clips need a free Pexels/Pixabay key.
kicker, caption, caption_em, video (file/URL) or video_query, image_query
presenter
A person: round photo, name, role, points.
name, role, photo, points[{text, cue}]
stats
Animated bars + "1 in N" people callout.
label, bars[{name, value, display, cue}], callout{em, text, people, empty, cue, empty_cue}
intro
Big logo, tagline, 0–2 photos, audience tags.
logo_cue, tagline, tagline_em, tagline_cue, photos[], tags[{text, cue, highlight}]
steps
2–4 numbered steps on a timeline with chips.
title, steps[{title, chips[], cue}]
flip
A card that gets stamped, then flips to the fix.
title, title_em, front{kicker, title, rows[{k, v, style}], stamp, cue}, back{kicker, title, rows[{k, v, style, cue}]} · style: alert, ok, primary
question
"So what makes X different?"
pre, em, post, em_cue, mark_cue
compare
Up to 4 rows: others vs you.
left_label, right_label, rows[{left, right, left_cue, right_cue}]
end
Optional shrinking bar chart, logo, tagline, URL.
chart{label, bars[{label, from, to}]}, tagline, tagline_em, logo_cue, tagline_cue
website
The real site scrolling in a phone.
url, kicker, title
lines (every scene)
What the voice says in that scene.
[{say, sub, seed, voice, pause_after}] · say is spoken, sub is the subtitle (defaults to say). Spell names how they sound, e.g. "Docs, Docs". voice picks a speaker from "voices" for dialogues. Change seed to re-roll a line.
any scene
Optional background: image_query (photo search), photo (file/URL), video or video_query (footage).
cue words
A word from that scene's lines. The animation fires the moment the voice says it.
  1. From a topic: type a topic and press Make the video. The AI writes the script (saved as videos/<topic>/video.json so you can edit it later) and the workflow builds the video.
  2. Write my own: fill in the form and script, then press Create on GitHub. GitHub opens with videos/<folder>/video.json filled in; press Commit changes.
  3. The commit starts the Make video workflow in the Actions tab. It records the voice (Kokoro, Microsoft Edge neural voices, Piper or Chatterbox, all free), times every animation to the spoken words, adds music and sound design and renders the MP4.
  4. About 5–10 minutes for a new video; voice lines are cached in the repo, so re-renders after visual edits are faster.
  5. The finished MP4, its subtitles (.srt) and a thumbnail appear in the Gallery here and under Releases on GitHub.
  6. To change a video, edit its video.json on GitHub and commit. To rebuild without changes: Actions → Make video → Run workflow.
